# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Morize is an open-source technical project. Contributors, maintainers, users, and community participants are expected to communicate professionally and respectfully.

Constructive behavior includes:

- discussing evidence, designs, code, and outcomes rather than attacking people;
- welcoming technically relevant questions and good-faith disagreement;
- giving specific, actionable review feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy, security embargoes, and confidential vulnerability reports;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or deliberate disruption.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is acceptable. Personal abuse is not.

Reviews may reject a proposal for correctness, security, scope, evidence, maintenance, licensing, cost, or product reasons. Rejection of a change is not rejection of a contributor.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment or discriminatory conduct;
- personal insults or sustained hostile behavior;
- publishing private information without permission;
- sexualized or threatening conduct;
- deliberate spam or disruption;
- knowingly fabricating technical evidence;
- exposing secrets or vulnerability details in public when a private channel is appropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or moderate project interactions that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md`.

For other conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel when possible.

## Scope

This policy applies to official Morize project spaces and to public conduct representing the project when that conduct materially affects project participation or safety.

## Principle

Morize values rigorous engineering and rigorous evidence. Both work better when people can disagree without hostility.
