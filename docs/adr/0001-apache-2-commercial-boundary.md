# ADR-0001 — Apache-2.0 Core with Optional Commercial Services

**Status:** Accepted for foundation planning  
**Date:** 2026-09-19

## Context

Morize is intended to be open source while preserving the option to charge users for future operated services. The founder's cost constraint concerns mandatory founder spend during pre-revenue development; it is not a user-free-forever requirement.

## Decision

- License the Morize repository under Apache License 2.0.
- Maintain local/self-hosted operation as a first-class product path.
- Permit future paid Morize-managed services.
- Keep billing/entitlements outside canonical memory semantics.
- Do not promise free hosted service in open-source product contracts.
- Require explicit cost ownership for any mandatory founder-paid dependency.

## Consequences

Positive:
- clear permissive commercial-friendly license;
- explicit patent grant/termination terms from Apache-2.0;
- self-hosting remains viable;
- managed-service monetization remains possible.

Trade-offs:
- Apache-2.0 permits third-party commercial redistribution subject to its terms;
- business advantage must come from execution, brand, service, ecosystem, reliability, and product quality rather than source exclusivity;
- trademark governance becomes a separate concern.

## Revisit triggers

- proposed license change;
- dual-licensing proposal;
- proprietary extension strategy;
- corporate restructuring requiring a different contribution agreement.
